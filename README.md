## About Me

- Software Engineer based in Islamabad, building across web, mobile, and hardware
- Mobile app developer at **Machadev**, shipping Flutter apps wired to in house firmware over BLE, WiFi, and UART (ESP32, STM32)
- Freelance full stack and product work under my **Crown Tech** brand
- Final year Software Engineering student at Air University Islamabad
- Currently deep in **LLM and RAG pipelines**, dynamic embedded UI on DWIN displays, and in app purchase flows
- I like the whole loop: firmware on the board, API in the middle, app in the hand
